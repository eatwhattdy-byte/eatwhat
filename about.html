ABOUT

Why this site exists

The Exchange Desk started from a small frustration: most currency converters give you a number and nothing else — no sense of whether it's a good time to convert, why the rate moved, or what a "fee-free" transfer is actually costing you.

This site pairs a live converter, built on European Central Bank reference data, with plain-English guides on the things that actually affect what you pay when money crosses a border: exchange rate fundamentals, travel money logistics, and the hidden margins in remittance pricing.

What we do

  • Publish a live, no-markup currency converter and rate grid.
  • Write guides that explain the reasoning behind exchange rates, not just the number.
  • Compare the real, all-in cost of common ways to move money internationally.

What we don't do

  • We don't provide financial, tax, or investment advice.
  • We don't process transfers or hold funds — this is an information site, not a money transfer service.
  • We don't accept payment to favor one provider over another in our guides.

If you have a question, a correction, or a topic you'd like covered, see the contact page.
