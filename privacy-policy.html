Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Exchange Desk ("we," "us," "this site") handles information when you visit. Replace the bracketed placeholders below with your actual details before publishing.

Information we collect

This site does not require an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name or email address, except when you choose to contact us directly (see our Contact page). Standard technical information — such as browser type, approximate location, and pages visited — may be collected automatically through cookies and analytics tools described below.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site uses cookies to remember basic preferences (such as your last-selected currencies) and, where advertising is enabled, to support ad delivery and measurement.

Live exchange rate data

Currency conversion figures are retrieved in real time from a third-party exchange rate API when you use the converter or rate grid. No personal information is transmitted as part of these requests beyond standard technical data included in any web request (such as IP address, used only to route the request).

Your choices

You can disable cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect some site functionality, such as remembering your preferred currencies. You can also opt out of personalized advertising through the Google Ads Settings link above, or through the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact page.
